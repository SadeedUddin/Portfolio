Ofqual-Regulated Qualifications

All our diplomas are awarded by Highfield Qualifications and regulated by Ofqual (Office of Qualifications and Examinations Regulation), placing them on the UK Regulated Qualifications Framework (RQF).

01

RQF Level 3 Qualifications

Equivalent to A-Levels / Advanced Technical Certificates. Designed for emerging team leaders, new managers, and trainers entering leadership roles.

  • Highfield Level 3 Diploma in Management (RQF)
  • Highfield Level 3 Award in Education & Training (AET)
02

RQF Level 4 Qualifications

Equivalent to Higher National Certificate (HNC) level. Ideal for operational managers, departmental heads, and experienced educators seeking advanced strategic knowledge.

  • Highfield Level 4 Diploma in Management & Leadership (RQF)
  • Highfield Level 4 Certificate in Education & Training (CET)
03

RQF Level 5 Qualifications

Equivalent to Foundation Degree / Undergraduate Diploma level. Created for senior managers, directors, and organizational leaders driving business growth and strategy.

  • Highfield Level 5 Diploma in Leadership & Management (RQF)
  • Highfield Level 5 Diploma in Education & Training (DET)
